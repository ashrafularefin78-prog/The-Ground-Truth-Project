Subject: Waiting times at this counter are not being measured

I am starting a hand-collected study of waiting times at this counter, because nothing about the queue is currently written down: not the length, not the waiting time, not how many people give up and leave.

The plan is to record 20 waits by hand, publish the raw rows and the photos, and report what the numbers can and cannot show.

If a target or a standard for waiting time already exists, I would like to quote it correctly.

What to include when you report it

  • The place, and the hour at which you saw the queue at its worst.
  • How long you waited yourself, in minutes. A rough count is far more useful than none.
  • Whether you saw anybody give up and leave.
  • The date, so the report can be matched to a row in data/observations.csv.
