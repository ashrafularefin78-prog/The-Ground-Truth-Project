The raw files

These are the files the site is built from. Download one, change a number, run the build, and every chart and sentence changes with it.

  • observations.csv — one row per wait
  • photos.csv — the photo record, with captions and alt text
  • stats.json — every figure on this site, with the row ids behind it

How to rebuild this site

Two commands, no internet connection and no account needed:

py tools/build.py
py tools/check.py
