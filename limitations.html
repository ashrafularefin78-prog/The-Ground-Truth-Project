Limitations — Ground Truth Project

This section is part of the study rather than a sentence bolted on at the end. Everything below is produced by the build from the same rows as the charts, so it changes when the data changes.

What that means for these numbers

  • Only 0 of the target 20 observations are logged, so 20 more rows are still needed before the figures settle down.
  • 0 of the target 8 photos exist, so 8 are still missing.

What this data cannot prove

  • This data shows how long people waited. It cannot show why. The bottleneck could be staffing, a till, a form, or a delivery that arrives late.
  • The rows describe 0 places at the hours I could be there. They say nothing about waiting times anywhere else.
  • There is no baseline. No queue was measured before any change was made, and nothing here is compared against a standard such as 15 minutes.
  • Weather and the academic calendar were not controlled. A rainy week or a holiday week can move a queue far more than anything the counter does.
  • Every row was collected by one person watching one queue at a time, so two slow counters at once could not both be counted.
  • I decided when to stand in the queue. Quiet hours and quiet days are certainly under-represented, so this is not a random sample of all waiting times.

What would make this study stronger

  • More observations at the places that have fewer than 3 timed waits, so their medians stop being indicative only.
  • The same places measured at the same hours on the same weekdays, so a comparison holds the day of the week still.
  • A second person counting alongside me, so a mistake in my own timing shows up as a difference instead of staying invisible.
  • A count of the people who walk past, see the queue and leave without ever joining it.

The method, the problems I ran into and the changes I made are written up in docs/method-note.md.
